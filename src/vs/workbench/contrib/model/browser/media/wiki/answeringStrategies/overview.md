# Ask Pollis Strategies — Overview

## What Is an Answering Strategy?
An answering strategy is the way Ask Pollis turns a question into an answer. Ask Pollis first searches its documents for passages, then may rerank the passages found, and then asks the answering model with the best passages. A strategy sets each of those steps: how many passages come from the panel a question is asked in, whether the reranker of Set Active Models chooses among more passages, and what the answering model is told about the panel. The answering model, the embedding model and the reranker stay those of Set Active Models, whichever strategy is in use. Two strategies differ only in how they use them, so they can be compared on the same questions. Asked in a panel, every strategy searches the question after the panel's title, as "Check a Convergence Order: What does this panel check?", so that "this panel" is searched as the panel it means.

## Which Strategy Does Ask Pollis Use?
Ask Pollis uses the strategy of the setting `pollis.ask.strategy`, Panel Aware unless another is chosen. The strategy is chosen in three places, which all change the same setting: Use in the strategy's row of the Ask Pollis Strategies page, the Strategy menu under the question box of Ask Pollis, and the Settings editor. In the table, the strategy in use says In Use in the column Ask Pollis, and every other strategy has Use there. A strategy whose file is removed or no longer passes the check is replaced by Panel Aware until the file is corrected.

## What Does the Base Strategy Do?
The Base strategy is Pollis' original strategy, the one the other strategies are compared with. Asked in a panel, Base takes five of every eight passages from the panel's wikis and the vignettes of the panel's packages, and the rest from the documents Ask Pollis searches without a panel. When a reranker is set in Set Active Models, Base finds three times as many passages and the reranker keeps the best. Base tells the answering model the panel's name and menu, so that "this panel" in a question has a meaning. Asked outside any panel, Base searches the collections nearest the question, as Ask Pollis always does.

## What Does the Panel Aware Strategy Do?
The Panel Aware strategy is the strategy Ask Pollis uses unless another is chosen. Panel Aware differs from Base only for questions asked in a panel. Panel Aware takes six of every eight passages from the panel's wikis and the vignettes of its packages when the panel's passages are near the question, and two when they are not, so a question about another subject is not held to the panel. When a reranker is set in Set Active Models, Panel Aware asks the reranker the question as asked in the panel, by the panel's name. Panel Aware tells the answering model the panel's name and menu, and what the panel holds: its models, Key Points, decision table, inputs and packages.

## What Does the Table of the Ask Pollis Strategies Page Show?
The table of the Ask Pollis Strategies page has one row for each strategy. Strategy gives its name and whose it is: Pollis, or Yours with the name of its file, and its author when it has one. What It Does says in one sentence how the strategy uses the panel a question is asked in. Panel FAQ Score gives the best model's score in the latest run of Evaluate Answering Models with the strategy on the Panel FAQ, with the model, the number of questions and the date, or says why there is no score. Ask Pollis says In Use for the strategy Ask Pollis uses.

## What Do the Details of a Strategy Show?
A click on a row of the Ask Pollis Strategies table shows the strategy's details below the table; a second click hides them, and a click on another row shows that one's instead. The details give the strategy's description, its Steps in plain words, and Needs: what the strategy relies on and whether the computer has it, a reranker for a rerank step, and passages embedded by meaning for a search that depends on how near the panel's passages are. Latest Runs lists the last three runs with the strategy and each model's score. The buttons at the end of the details act on that strategy: Evaluate..., Copy as New..., and, for a strategy of yours, Open File and Propose to the Catalogue...

## How Are Strategies Installed from GitHub?
The catalogue of answering strategies is the GitHub repository `Trumpingtons/pollis-strategies`. When the catalogue offers strategies that are not installed, the Ask Pollis Strategies page lists them under From GitHub, each with What It Does and Install. Install writes the strategy's file to `~/.pollis/ask/strategies/`, and the strategy then has a row in the table, like one written by hand. When the catalogue holds another version of a strategy installed, the strategy's details offer Update from the Catalogue, which asks before replacing the file. Without a connection, From GitHub is not shown, and the strategies installed keep working.
