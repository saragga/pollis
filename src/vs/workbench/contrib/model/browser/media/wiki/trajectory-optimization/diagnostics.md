# Diagnostics: Trajectory Optimization

## Convergence Failure

Check cost decreasing per iteration. If plateau, may need better initialization or adjusted parameters.

## Constraint Violation

Monitor feasibility: verify dynamics constraints and inequality bounds satisfied.

## Solution Quality

Compare cost across different solvers. High variation suggests local optima or numerical issues.

## Sensitivity

Test trajectory robustness to parameter perturbations and unmodeled disturbances.
