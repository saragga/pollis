# RSS Feeds — Overview

## What the RSS Feeds Page Shows
The **RSS Feeds** page is Pollis's feed reader. On the left, the feeds are listed by topic: those of Pollis's catalogue, followed or not, and those added on the Add Feed page. On the right, the items of the feed or topic chosen, or of **All Feeds**, are listed newest first, with a summary, a thumbnail for a video, and the feed's name and the item's age. Opening an item opens it in the internal browser and marks it read; **Mark Unread** marks it unread again. Pollis reads the feeds every hour while it is open, and **Refresh** reads them at once. The newest unread items of the feeds chosen for it also appear in the RSS box of the welcome page.

## The Feeds Column: Subscribed, Here and Welcome Page
Each feed, and each topic, has three ticks, red when off and green when on, half and half for a topic whose feeds differ. **Subscribed** says whether Pollis reads the feed: an unsubscribed feed is no longer read, but its items stay, and ticking Subscribed on a catalogue feed follows it. **Here** says whether its items are listed on the page; a feed not displayed here is not on the welcome page either, but is still read and archived. **Welcome Page** says whether its newest unread items appear in the welcome page's RSS box. **Unread / Total** counts the feed's items in the period of Items From. A topic opens and closes with its chevron, and the chevron of All Feeds closes them all, then restores them as they were.

## Following and Removing Feeds
The catalogue offers feeds in about thirty topics, from Julia, Statistics, Economics and Central Banks, and Finance Research to Law, History and Astronomy and Space, listed unsubscribed until they are followed. **Add Feed** opens the Add Feed page, for a feed the catalogue does not have: an address, a journal, a website, a YouTube channel, a PubMed or arXiv search, or email alerts. Pointing at a feed shows **Remove**; a feed removed moves to Removed Feeds on the Add Feed page, where Restore brings it back as it was.

## The Archive of Items in PollisRSS
Every item Pollis receives is also kept in an archive, which the PollisRSS database of the Databases view reads with SQL. The page itself keeps only the latest items of each feed, while the archive keeps every item since the feed was first followed. The items added to Zotero and the podcast episodes downloaded are in the same database, in its zotero and episodes views.
