# Replications — Copies and Updates

## Where Your Copy of a Replication Is Kept
Clicking a name in Replications makes your own copy of the notebook in `~/.pollis/workflows` the first time, and opens the copy after that. The copy is yours to change: Pollis does not write over it, and your changes do not touch the library.

## What Happens When a Replication Is Updated
The list of replications is updated between releases of Pollis. A replication marked **New** has just arrived, and **Updated** means a newer version exists. When your copy is behind, the page says **Your copy is older than this version.** and **Open Updated Version** opens the latest one; your own copy stays where it is.

## Reading the Size Column of Replications
The **Size** column gives the space the replication takes and the number of files. A figure marked **download** adds the data the notebook fetches when it runs, and one marked **download if asked** adds data it fetches only if you choose to run that part.
