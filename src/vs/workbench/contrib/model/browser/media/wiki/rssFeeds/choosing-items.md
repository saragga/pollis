# RSS Feeds — Choosing the Items Listed

## What Decides the Items Listed on the RSS Feeds Page
Four things decide which items the right of the RSS Feeds page lists: the feed or topic chosen on the left, or All Feeds; the period of **Items From**; the choice of **Journals**; and the feeds whose **Here** tick is off, which are left out. A line above the items says how many there are and which of these choices narrow them, such as the period, the Journals choice and how many feeds are not displayed here. The items come in pages, of a number of rows chosen below them, with **Previous** and **Next**.

## Items From: the Period and On a Day
**Items From** chooses the period of the items listed and counted: The Last 7 Days, The Last 30 Days, The Last 90 Days, or All Items Kept. **On a Day...** opens a calendar, where the days with items are in bold, and lists the items of the day chosen. Only the latest items of each feed are kept on the page, and the archive goes back only to the day the feed was first followed, as a feed cannot tell what it published before then. The Unread / Total counts of the feeds column follow the period.

## Journals: Filtering by the Level and Metrics of a Journal
An item from a journal carries a tag with the journal's level and metrics. The levels are those of the Norwegian register of scientific journals, built into Pollis: level 1 for academic journals and level 2 for the leading journals of a field. **Journals** narrows the items to those of some journals, while items not from a journal always stay:

| Choice | Keeps the items of |
|---|---|
| All | Every journal |
| Academic (Level 1 or 2) | The journals the Norwegian register ranks as academic |
| Leading (Level 2) | The leading journals of their field |
| Open Access | Journals whose articles are all open access (OpenAlex), or listed in the Directory of Open Access Journals |
| SJR Q1 and SJR Q1–Q2 | Journals in the top quarter, or half, of SJR (SCImago) in a category, once imported |
| SNIP Above 1 | Journals cited more than the average of their field (CWTS), once imported |

The button beside Journals, and a click on a tag, open the Journal Metrics page, which explains each value and imports more metrics.
