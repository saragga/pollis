# RSS Feeds — Zotero, OPML and Podcast Episodes

## Adding an Item of a Feed to Zotero
Pointing at an item of the RSS Feeds page shows **Add to Zotero**. It needs Zotero 7 or later open on the same computer, as Pollis adds the item through the server that Zotero runs for its browser connectors. A list of Zotero's libraries and collections opens, with the one selected in Zotero first, or the one used last; the item goes to the one chosen, and its line then reads **In Zotero**. When Zotero is not open, or busy, a message says so and nothing is added. The items added are also listed in the zotero view of the PollisRSS database.

## Exporting the Feeds as OPML
**Export as OPML** saves the feeds followed as an OPML file, the format feed readers use to exchange lists of feeds. In Zotero, File > New Feed > From OPML... follows them all from that file, and other feed readers import it the same way. PubMed searches and email alerts are left out of the file, since only Pollis reads them.

## Videos and Podcast Episodes
A feed of videos, such as a YouTube channel, or of a podcast is marked in the feeds column. An item of such a feed has **Watch** or **Listen**, which opens the video's page or the episode's audio in the internal browser. A podcast episode can also be kept: **Download Episode** saves its file to the episodes folder of Pollis's RSS folder, and then reads **Downloaded**, which shows where the file is. The episodes downloaded are listed in the episodes view of the PollisRSS database.
