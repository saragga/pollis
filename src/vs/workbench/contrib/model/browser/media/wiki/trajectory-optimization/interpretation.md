# Interpretation: Trajectory Optimization Results

## Trajectory Shape

Optimal trajectory reflects cost weights and dynamics constraints. Smooth sections indicate high control cost; rapid transitions suggest aggressive objectives.

## Control Effort

Total control energy reveals feasibility. High effort may signal infeasible specifications or poor weighting.

## Convergence Behavior

Iteration count and cost reduction indicate solver efficiency. Fast convergence suggests well-conditioned problem.

## Robustness

Sensitivity to parameter changes reveals problem structure and solution stability.
