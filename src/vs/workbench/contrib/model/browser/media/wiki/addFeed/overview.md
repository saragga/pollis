# Add Feed — Overview

## What the Add Feed Page Does
The **Add Feed** page follows a source that Pollis's catalogue of feeds does not have. It has four sections. **From an Address** follows an RSS or Atom feed, a journal's table of contents, a website's feed or a YouTube channel. **From a Search** turns a PubMed or arXiv search into a feed. **From Email Alerts** turns the alerts that Google Scholar, JSTOR and other sources send by email into feeds. **Your Feeds** lists the feeds added on this page, by topic, with those removed. The items of every feed followed are read every hour and listed on the RSS Feeds page; the newest unread ones of the feeds chosen there also appear in the RSS box of the welcome page.

## Adding a Feed From an Address
The address in **From an Address** can be one of four kinds. An RSS or Atom feed is followed as it is. A journal's page at Wiley, Taylor & Francis, SAGE, Elsevier, Cambridge Core or Oxford Academic leads Pollis to the journal's table of contents. Another website's page leads to the feeds the page lists, and when it lists several, the page asks which one to follow. A YouTube channel's address, its @handle or its id leads to the channel's feed of new videos. **Find** looks for the feed and shows its newest items, so that the feed can be checked before it is followed. **Name** and **Topic** say how the feed is listed on the RSS Feeds page; **New Topic...** makes a topic of your own. **Add Feed** follows it.

## Search Feeds From Other Services
Some services turn a search into a feed behind a button of their own, and the address of that feed goes in From an Address. In EBSCOhost, run the search, then Share above the results, and under Create an alert, RSS Feed. In ProQuest, run the search, then Save search/alert at the top of the results, and Get RSS feed. Both need a library's access. Google Alerts, at google.com/alerts and signed in, delivers an alert as an RSS feed when Deliver to is set to RSS feed under Show options. NBER and RePEc NEP publish feeds by programme and by report rather than by search; they are in the catalogue, on the RSS Feeds page.

## Your Feeds and Removed Feeds
**Your Feeds** lists the feeds added on the Add Feed page, by topic, each with **Remove**. A feed removed moves to **Removed Feeds**, below, and **Restore** brings it back as it was, with its topic and name. The feeds of Pollis's catalogue are not listed here: they are followed with the Subscribed box of the RSS Feeds page, and removed there too. **Back: RSS Feeds**, under the page's title, opens the RSS Feeds page, where the items of the feeds are read.
