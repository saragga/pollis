# Discover Literature — Factsheet

**Discover Literature** finds the most cited articles on a topic in OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar, and shows how they stand against the articles of their field and year.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Find the articles that matter on a topic, and keep the right ones |
| **Search by** | Topic, Field, Years, Citations At Least, Influence, Journal Ranking, Only Articles Anyone Can Read |
| **Columns** | Select, Year, Article, Journal; under OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar: Since Publishing, Last n Years, Influential, FWCI, Percentile |
| **Actions** | See in References, Show in Panel..., Copy BibTeX, Open PDFs, Snowball Search..., Save to PollisReferences |
| **Keys** | Optional for both indexes, kept in the system's keychain |

## When to Use Discover Literature
- Starting on a topic, and seeing its most cited articles.
- Telling an article with many citations from one that stands out in its field and year.
- Picking the seeds of a Snowball Search.
- Saving articles to PollisReferences, or copying their BibTeX.

## What Discover Literature Does Not Do
The Discover Literature page lists at most what the indexes return for a topic, the most cited first, so a recent or little-cited article may not appear. It does not read the articles, and a citation count is not a judgement of quality. Nothing is saved until you choose Save to PollisReferences, or confirm in Show in Panel...
