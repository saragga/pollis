# RSS Feeds — Factsheet

**RSS Feeds** reads the feeds Pollis follows, every hour, and lists their items by topic and feed, newest first.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Read the news, the new articles and the new episodes of the feeds followed |
| **Feeds** | Pollis's catalogue by topic, and the feeds added on the Add Feed page |
| **Ticks** | Subscribed, Here and Welcome Page, by feed or by topic |
| **Items From** | The Last 7, 30 or 90 Days, All Items Kept, or On a Day... |
| **Journals** | All, Academic (Level 1 or 2), Leading (Level 2), Open Access; SJR Q1, SJR Q1–Q2 and SNIP Above 1 once imported |
| **Item actions** | Open, Mark Unread, Watch or Listen, Download Episode, Add to Zotero |
| **Page actions** | Add Feed, Refresh, Export as OPML |
| **Archive** | The PollisRSS database of the Databases view |
| **Menu** | Explore > RSS > RSS Feeds; the gear of the RSS box on the welcome page |

## When to Use RSS Feeds
- Following the new issues of the journals of a field, filtered to its leading journals.
- Reading a central bank's releases or a working paper series as they appear.
- Watching a channel's new lectures or listening to a podcast's new episodes.
- Sending an article found in a feed to a Zotero collection.

## What RSS Feeds Does Not Do
The RSS Feeds page lists what each feed publishes: a title, a summary and a link. It does not fetch the full text of an article, which opens at its publisher in the internal browser. A feed tells only what it publishes from the day it is followed, so the page cannot show the items of an earlier period.
