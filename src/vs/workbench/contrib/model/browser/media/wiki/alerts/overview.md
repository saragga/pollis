# Alerts — Overview

## What Alerts Do in Pollis
An alert tells you when something you follow meets a condition. Pollis has three kinds. An alert on **A Quote** watches a symbol at Yahoo Finance, an index, a share, a future or a currency, and Pollis looks its value up every minute while it is open; the quotes are delayed by up to about 20 minutes, depending on the exchange. An alert on **SEC Filings** watches the filings of one company, several, an industry or every company at the SEC's EDGAR, every 15 minutes. An alert on **Economic Data** watches a series at FRED, the ECB, the IMF or the World Bank, every hour. When an alert fires, Pollis shows a notification of its severity, and the alert appears in the Alerts tab of the welcome page and under Fired Alerts.

## The Three Pages: Add Alert, Alerts and Quote
**Add Alert** is a form: Alert On (A Quote, SEC Filings or Economic Data), then the symbol, company or series, the condition and its value, **Shows As** (Information, Warning or Error) and **Fires** (Every Time, again each time the condition is met anew, or Once, paused once it fires). Beside the form, a chart shows the recent values with the alert's value drawn across it, or the company's recent filings. **Alerts** lists Your Alerts and Fired Alerts. **Quote** shows a symbol's latest value, its previous close, its day and 52-week ranges and its volume, with a chart on which the alerts on it are drawn, and **Add Alert** for it.

## Your Alerts and Fired Alerts
**Your Alerts** lists every alert with its latest value, each with **Pause** or **Resume** and **Remove**. A click on an alert's symbol opens its quote and chart in a panel under it, one at a time; a filing or a series opens at its source. **Open alerts.json** opens the file in which the alerts are kept. **Fired Alerts** lists the alerts that fired, newest first: **Mark Read** and **Mark All Read** keep them listed as read, **Dismiss** takes one out, and **Clear** takes them all out. The unread ones are those the welcome page shows.
