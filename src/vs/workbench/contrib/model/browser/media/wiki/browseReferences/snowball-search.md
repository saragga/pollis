# Browse References — Citation Searches

## Why Follow Citations From Works You Already Have
A library of references can grow by following citations, not only by searching with keywords. In an audit of one systematic review, [Greenhalgh and Peacock (2005)](#ref-GP2005a) traced 51% of its sources to pursuing references of references, and 30% to the searches set out in the protocol. The method has guidelines of its own, known as snowballing ([Wohlin (2014)](#ref-Woh2014a)), in which the reference lists and citing works of a starting set lead to new candidates.

## Where a Citation Search Starts in Pollis
Compose > Literature Radar > Snowball Search Map takes works in PollisReferences as its seeds, by their title, author or key. The works it finds are kept in the map and its record, not in PollisReferences. A work found there joins Browse References, with the source User, once it is saved from Discover Literature or Import References.
