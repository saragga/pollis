# Journal Metrics — Overview

## What the Journal Metrics Page Is For
The **Journal Metrics** page gathers the sources of the levels and metrics that Pollis attaches to journals. They tag the items of journals on the RSS Feeds page and on the welcome page, narrow those items with the **Journals** filter, and fill the metrics views of the PollisJournals database. Two sources are built into Pollis. The other five are not free to share: each is downloaded by you, for your own use, at its source's site, and Pollis imports the file on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. The page opens from Explore > RSS > Journal Metrics, from the button beside Journals on the RSS Feeds page, from the tag of a journal item, and from PollisJournals in the Databases view.

## The Three Groups of Sources
The page lists the sources in three groups, each with a card per source: what it measures, a table of its metrics with their values and meaning, who can use it, and how to download its file.

| Group | Sources | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Built into Pollis** | Norwegian Register, OpenAlex | Nothing: open data, updated every month with PollisJournals.jl |
| **Free to Download** | SCImago Journal Rank (SJR), CWTS Journal Indicators (SNIP), ABDC Journal Quality List | Download the file at the source's site, no account needed |
| **With an Account or a Subscription** | Scopus (CiteScore), Journal Citation Reports (Impact Factor) | Sign in at the source, often through a university library, then download |

The badge of each card says **Built in**, **Not imported** or **Imported**.

## Import File..., Download Page and Remove
**Download Page** on a card opens the source's download page in Pollis's own browser, beside the page. **Import File...**, at the top of the page or on a card, imports a downloaded file: Pollis recognises the source from the file's columns, whichever card the button is on. Under each card, the files imported are listed with their latest year and when they were imported, each with **Remove**, which deletes Pollis's copy and leaves the downloaded file where it is.

## Ask an Agent
**Ask an Agent** starts your coding agent on the Journal Metrics skill, listed in Compose > Agent Skills. The agent helps choose the metrics that suit your field, download them and import them, and then answers questions about journals with SQL on PollisJournals: which quartile a journal is in, whether it is rated A* on the ABDC list, or how several journals compare. **Back: RSS Feeds** returns to the RSS Feeds page.
