# Sell One Product

Your team sells one product. Rival teams sell the same kind of product, and the same buyers choose between all of you, or buy none.

## The Game

- The game lasts three rounds. Each round your team sets its price and its marketing, and the facilitator plays the round once every team has submitted.
- Each unit costs you 4 € to make, and you pay 2,000 € a round however many you sell, plus what you spend on marketing.
- A higher price earns more on each unit but loses buyers to the other teams. Marketing wins buyers, but each euro more wins fewer than the one before.
- The buyers would buy between 8,000 and 12,000 units a round, and their demand varies from one round to the next.
- After each round everyone sees every team's price, marketing, market share and profit. Only your team sees how many units the buyers would buy, its sales, revenue and costs.
- The team with the highest cumulative profit after three rounds wins.
- During the game the facilitator can release news, which can change your costs from that round on.
