# Sell One Product

Your team sells one product. Rival teams sell the same kind of product, and the same buyers choose between all of you, or buy none.

## The Game

- The game lasts three rounds. Each round your team sets its price, and the facilitator plays the round once every team has submitted.
- Each unit costs you 4 € to make, and you pay 2,000 € a round however many you sell.
- A higher price earns more on each unit but loses buyers to the other teams.
- After each round everyone sees every team's price, market share and profit. Only your team sees its sales, revenue and costs.
- The team with the highest cumulative profit after three rounds wins.
- During the game the facilitator can release news, which can change your costs from that round on.
